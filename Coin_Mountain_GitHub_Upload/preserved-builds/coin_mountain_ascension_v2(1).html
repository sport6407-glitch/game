First-person 3D idle adventure

COIN MOUNTAIN:
ASCENSION

A mountain of 100,000 coins stands before you. Scoop it by hand, then commit to one of three upgrade paths — Handcraft, Machinery or Magnetics — and build drones that mine while you explore. Clear the mountain to Ascend for a permanent bonus and a brand-new biome.

Controls: WASD to walk, mouse to look, Space to jump, hold E (or the left mouse button) near the mountain to collect, B for upgrades. Golden coins appear around the world — follow the light beams. If mouse capture is blocked, click and drag to look around.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Requires an internet connection to load the Three.js 3D engine.
