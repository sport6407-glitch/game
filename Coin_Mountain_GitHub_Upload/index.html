First-person 3D idle adventure

COIN MOUNTAIN:
ASCENSION

A 1,500,000-coin mountain towers over the valley. Collect, bank, and upgrade across six paths without being locked into a single build. Equip different tools, unleash cooldown-based ultimate abilities, build a drone fleet, and clear the mountain to Ascend for permanent bonuses and new biomes.

Controls: WASD to walk, mouse to look, Space to jump, hold E (or the left mouse button) near the mountain to collect, B for upgrades, Q to cycle owned tools, and 1–6 to trigger each unlocked path’s ultimate. Golden coins appear around the world — follow the light beams. If mouse capture is blocked, click and drag to look around.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. The 3D engine is bundled with the game.
